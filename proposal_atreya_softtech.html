06 · Payment Architecture

Razorpay Integration Flow

How payments work end-to-end on your website — from customer click to money in your bank.

👆
1. Customer Selects Plan

Clicks "Get Started" on your Pricing page

📋
2. Enter Details

Name, email, phone in payment modal

🖥️
3. Order Created

Server calls Razorpay API, gets order ID

💳
4. Razorpay Checkout

Secure payment popup (UPI/Card/NB)

✅
5. Verify & Confirm

Signature verified on server, DB updated

🏦
6. Settlement

Razorpay settles to your bank (T+3)

⚠️ Razorpay KYC Required for Live Payments Test mode works immediately with test keys. To accept real money, you must submit business documents (GST certificate, PAN, bank account details, business proof) and complete Razorpay's KYC verification. Typically takes 3–7 business days. Atreya SoftTech will assist with the integration — KYC is done by the client directly with Razorpay.
