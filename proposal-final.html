Executive Summary

Why Choose WordPress?

This proposal covers the design, development, and deployment of a complete corporate website on WordPress CMS with a fully custom Bootstrap 5 theme — no page-builder lock-in. The result is a fast, SEO-optimised, easily maintainable website that the client team can update without developer involvement.

📦 Delivery Platform

WordPress 6.x · Custom Bootstrap 5 PHP theme (no page builder) · Hostinger Business Shared Hosting (auto-SSL, LiteSpeed cache) · Razorpay payment gateway.

Payment Gateway

Razorpay Transaction Fees

Razorpay is integrated as the payment gateway for subscription/plan payments. There are no setup fees — costs are purely transaction-based, charged per payment collected. These are variable and paid directly to Razorpay.

ℹ️ How Razorpay Charges Work

Razorpay deducts its Transaction Discount Rate (TDR) automatically from each payment before transferring the balance to your bank account. You do not receive a separate invoice — the fee is deducted at source.

Payment MethodTDR RateNotes
Account Setup—FREE — No setup fee for standard accounts
Domestic Cards (Debit / Credit)2% per transactionStandard rate for Visa, Mastercard, RuPay
UPI / BHIM0% (currently)Razorpay currently charges 0% on UPI transactions
Net Banking2% per transactionAll major Indian banks supported
Wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, etc.)2% per transactionSubject to wallet provider terms
International Cards3% + GSTRequires prior Razorpay approval
EMI (Credit Card)3% per transactionBank EMI; no-cost EMI may vary
